# Privacy Policy for MouseKeeper

Effective date: April 27, 2026

MouseKeeper is a small Windows utility that runs locally on your device.

## Personal Data

MouseKeeper does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Because MouseKeeper does not collect or store personal data, there is no user data for the publisher to access, correct, export, or delete.

## Local Activity

MouseKeeper watches local mouse activity only so it can pause automatic mouse movement when you move the mouse yourself. This activity is used only while the app is running. It is not saved, logged, or sent anywhere.

## Network Access

MouseKeeper does not use user accounts, cloud services, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the support contact listed on the Microsoft Store page.
